# 🌐 Yedija Rafael | Personal Portfolio Website

## 📖 Project Overview

This is a single-page responsive portfolio website about me, Yedija Rafael, a Computer Science student at BINUS International who dreams of becoming a police officer in the cyber field. The website shows who I am through many small description boxes: my story, personality, skills, education, experience, projects, goals, values, interests, and contact details.

It is built with **HTML**, **CSS**, and a little **JavaScript**, all inside one `index.html` file.

## 📐 Layout & Sections

The page is split into six groups. Each scroll shows one group, and the previous group lifts up and fades away.

1. **Intro** – Name, role, short summary, menu buttons, and my photo
2. **About me & Personality** – My story, what drives me, and how I describe myself
3. **Skills, Education & Experience** – Negotiation, Python, networking, BINUS International, Taruna Nusantara Senior High School, Student Council (OSIS), PATAKA, and IELTS 7.0
4. **Projects, Goals & Currently learning** – My GitHub projects, my goals, and what I am studying now
5. **Values, Interests, Fun facts & Quote** – Integrity and honesty, running, and my Ironman ambition
6. **How I work, Languages & Contact** – My working style, languages, email, and social links

## ✨ Features

- **Scroll groups:** one relevant group of information at a time, with a lift-and-fade transition
- **Side navigation:** dots with labels on the right side that show the current group and jump to any group
- **Slow smooth scrolling** when using the menu buttons or the side navigation
- **Sticker-style photo** with a white outline that fits the design
- **Automatic dark mode** that follows the device settings
- **Social links** to LinkedIn, GitHub, and Instagram
